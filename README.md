# EssentialTimer - App Store Support & User Guide

EssentialTimer is a professional interval timer and audio-guided training application for iOS designed for freedivers, breathwork practitioners, fitness athletes, and everyday timing.

---

## Key Features

- **Apnea O2 & CO2 Tables**: Dedicated 16-round oxygen and carbon dioxide tolerance tables for freediving training.
- **10 HD Voice Guides**: Choose from 10 professional voice coaches (American & British accents) with seamless background music audio mixing.
- **Multi-Phase Workout Timers**: HIIT, Tabata, Boxing / BJJ Grappling, CrossFit WODs, AcroYoga, & Movement Flow protocols.
- **Custom Routine Builder**: Create and edit your own interval routines with custom work, rest, prepare, and cooldown durations.
- **History & Session Analytics**: Track completed sessions, rounds, completion rates, and physical contraction counts over time.
- **100% Private & Local**: All workout history, timer configurations, and logs stay safely stored locally on your device.
