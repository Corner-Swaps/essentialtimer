# ⏱️ EssentialTimer — Professional Interval Timer & Audio Guide for iOS

**EssentialTimer** is an intelligent, high-performance interval timer and audio-guided training application for iOS designed for freedivers, breathwork practitioners, fitness athletes, and everyday timing.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🫁 Apnea O₂ & CO₂ Tables**: Dedicated 16-round oxygen and carbon dioxide tolerance tables for freediving training.
- **🗣️ 10 HD Calm Studio Voice Guides**: Choose from 10 professional voice coaches (American & British accents) with seamless background music audio mixing.
- **⏱️ Multi-Phase Workout Timers**: HIIT, Tabata, Boxing / BJJ Grappling, CrossFit WODs, AcroYoga, & Movement Flow protocols.
- **🛠️ Custom Routine Builder**: Create and edit your own interval routines with custom work, rest, prepare, and cooldown durations.
- **📊 History & Session Analytics**: Track completed sessions, rounds, completion rates, and physical contraction counts over time.
- **🔒 100% Private & Local**: All workout history, timer configurations, and logs stay safely stored locally on your device.
