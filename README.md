## 📖 App Overview & User Guide

EssentialTimer is a high-performance, fully customizable timer and workout companion built for freedivers, breathwork practitioners, fitness athletes, and everyday timing.

### 1. Voice Guides & Sound Modes
- **Voice Cues**: Go to **Voice Cues** in the main menu to choose from 10 HD studio voice coaches (American & British accents).
- **Beep Mode / Silent Mode**: Use the audio toggle button on the active timer screen to switch between Voice Guides, Beep Mode, or Mute.
- **Background Audio**: EssentialTimer seamlessly plays voice cues over your background music (Spotify, Apple Music) without interrupting your playback.

### 2. Apnea O₂ & CO₂ Tables (Freediving)
- **O₂ Table (Oxygen Tolerance)**: Automatically increases hold duration by your selected increment every round while keeping rest constant.
- **CO₂ Table (Carbon Dioxide Tolerance)**: Automatically decreases rest duration by your selected decrement every round while keeping hold constant.
- **Contraction Counter**: Tap the physical contraction button during hold phases to log contractions into your history analytics.

### 3. Custom Routines & Favorites
- **Custom Workout Section**: Tap **Create Custom Workout Section** to build your own multi-phase interval routines with custom work, rest, prepare, and cooldown durations.
- **Favorites Tab**: Tap the **Star** icon on any category or custom routine to add it to your Favorites tab for 1-tap quick start access.
