Important Health & Safety Notice

Never practice breath-holding (apnea) in or near water without certified, direct one-on-one supervision. Practicing breath-hold tables in water without trained safety divers creates extreme risk of Shallow Water Blackout (SWB), Loss of Motor Control (LMC), and fatal drowning.

All apnea tables, breath-hold tests, and interval protocols in EssentialTimer are designed exclusively for dry, seated, or lying down practice on a soft, safe surface. Never use during machine operation or driving.
