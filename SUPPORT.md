## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I switch between Voice Guides and Beep Mode?
In the main menu, select **Voice Cues** to choose your favorite studio voice coach (10 HD voices available in American & British accents). During active workout timers, you can also toggle audio modes or mute audio directly from the control buttons.

### How do Apnea O₂ and CO₂ Tables work?
- **O₂ Table (Oxygen Tolerance)**: Automatically increases hold duration by your selected increment every round while keeping rest constant.
- **CO₂ Table (Carbon Dioxide Tolerance)**: Automatically decreases rest duration by your selected decrement every round while keeping hold constant.
- **Contraction Counter**: Tap the physical contraction button during hold phases to log physical contractions into your history analytics.

### Does EssentialTimer work while listening to music (Spotify/Apple Music)?
Yes! EssentialTimer automatically mixes voice cues and countdowns over your background music playback without interrupting your music.

### Is my workout data private?
Yes. 100% of your workout history, timer logs, and settings are saved locally on your device. We do not collect or track any personal data.
