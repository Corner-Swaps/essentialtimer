## ⚠️ Critical Health & Apnea Safety Warning

> ### DANGER: RISK OF HYPOXIA AND SHALLOW WATER BLACKOUT (SWB)
> - **NEVER PRACTICE BREATH-HOLDING (APNEA) IN OR NEAR WATER WITHOUT DIRECT, CERTIFIED ONE-ON-ONE SUPERVISION.** Practicing breath-holding in pools, oceans, lakes, or bathtubs without trained safety divers creates extreme risk of Shallow Water Blackout (SWB), Loss of Motor Control (LMC), and fatal drowning.
> - **DRY PRACTICE ONLY**: All apnea tables, breath-hold tests, and interval protocols in EssentialTimer are intended strictly for **dry, seated, or lying down practice on a soft, safe surface** (such as a yoga mat or bed).
> - **MEDICAL DISCLAIMER**: EssentialTimer is an athletic interval timer and does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a physician before undertaking rigorous interval training or hypoxia conditioning.

## 📖 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Is EssentialTimer completely free? Are there subscriptions?
Yes. EssentialTimer is **100% free**. There are zero in-app purchases, recurring subscription fees, auto-renewing charges, or locked features. No credit card or billing management is ever required.

### 2. Voice Guides, Beep Mode & Sound Settings
- **Voice Cues**: Go to **Voice Cues** in the main menu to choose from 10 HD studio voice coaches (American & British accents).
- **Beep Mode / Silent Mode**: Use the audio toggle button on the active timer screen to switch between Voice Prompts, Beep Mode, or Mute.
- **Background Music**: EssentialTimer seamlessly plays audio cues over your background music (Spotify, Apple Music, Podcasts) without stopping your playback.

### 3. Apnea O₂ & CO₂ Tables (Freediving)
- **O₂ Table (Oxygen Tolerance)**: Automatically increases the breath-hold duration by your selected increment every round while keeping rest intervals constant.
- **CO₂ Table (Carbon Dioxide Tolerance)**: Automatically decreases rest/breathing durations by your selected decrement every round while keeping hold durations constant.
- **Contraction Counter**: Tap the physical contraction button during hold phases to log contractions into your history analytics.

### 4. Custom Routines & Favorites
- **Custom Workout Section**: Tap **Create Custom Workout Section** to build your own multi-phase interval routines with custom work, rest, prepare, and cooldown durations.
- **Favorites Tab**: Tap the **Star** icon on any category or custom routine to add it to your Favorites tab for 1-tap quick start access.

### 5. History, Session Analytics & Data Portability
- **Session History**: All completed sessions are automatically logged into the **History** tab and organized on the **Calendar** view.
- **Data Export (DSAR)**: Tap **Export JSON** in the History tab to download a machine-readable JSON backup of your session logs and routines.
- **Instant Data Erasure**: In compliance with GDPR Art. 17 (Right to Erasure), tap **Privacy Policy & Terms → Clear Data** to permanently wipe all local history and custom workouts.
